Project Policy

The principles that govern how EtherWare is shared, used, and protected.

01Closed-Source Policy

EtherWare is a closed-source project. The internal structure, codebase, and design architecture are not publicly shared.

02Educational and Personal Use

EtherWare is intended for educational and personal experimentation purposes only. It is not designed for commercial deployment or enterprise-level distribution.

03Copying and Redistribution

Copying and resharing publicly available materials such as documentation or descriptive content is allowed.

04Modification Restriction

Modification of project components, branding, identity, or distributed materials requires explicit permission from the project owner. Unauthorized modification, repackaging, or distribution of altered versions is not permitted without approval.
