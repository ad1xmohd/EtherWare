About

What is External WiFi Intelligence?

A device-friendly approach to extending wireless capability.

External WiFi Intelligence allows supported devices to extend their wireless capabilities using compatible external WiFi adapters.

EtherWare focuses on reliability, flexibility, and device-friendly operation. It enables expanded wireless capability without requiring root access or developer mode activation.

Designed with safety in mind, EtherWare operates within normal device permissions and is built to maintain stability, performance, and compatibility.
